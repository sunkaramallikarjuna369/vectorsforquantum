Welcome to Vectors in Quantum Computing!

This interactive guide explores the fundamental role of vectors in quantum computing. Vectors are the mathematical foundation that represents quantum states, transformations, and measurements.

In quantum computing:

  • Quantum states are represented as vectors in complex Hilbert space
  • Quantum gates are unitary transformations acting on state vectors
  • Measurements are inner products between state vectors
  • Entanglement is represented through multi-qubit vector spaces

Each concept includes:

  • 🎨 Interactive 3D visualizations using Three.js
  • 🐍 Python code demonstrations with NumPy
  • 📊 Mathematical foundations and formulas
  • 🔬 Quantum computing applications
  • 💡 Intuitive explanations and examples
